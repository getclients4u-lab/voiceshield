# The Parent & Grandparent Conversation Guide

The people most targeted by voice-clone scams are also the hardest to reach.
Fear-based warnings make them anxious and defensive. This guide shows you
how to actually protect them — with love, not lectures.

---

## The One Sentence That Changes Everything

> *"Mom, I want us to have a code word — just for emergencies. If I ever
> call you in real trouble, I'll say it, and you'll know it's really me.
> And if someone calls claiming to be me and doesn't know the word, you'll
> know it's a fake and you can hang up safely. It's my job to look out for
> you, and this is how I do it."*

**Why this works:** it frames the protocol as *their child protecting them*,
not as *the world being scary*. It gives them a concrete job (remember the
word) instead of a vague fear.

---

## The Two-Question Method (Make It Their Idea)

**Step 1 — Open gently:**
*"Have you heard about those calls where they copy a voice? It's been in
the news a lot."*

Let them tell you what they know. Correct gently, don't lecture.

**Step 2 — Let them choose:**
*"Would it feel safer if we had a way to know it's really me every time?"*

Now it's their decision, not your lecture. People protect what they choose.

---

## The Three Rules (Keep It This Simple)

Older adults do best with exactly three rules. Don't add a fourth.

1. **The word.** *"We have one word. If I ever call in trouble, I'll say
   it. Anyone who can't say it is not me."*
2. **The call-back.** *"If someone asks for money, hang up and call me
   back on MY number."*
3. **The group.** *"You're in the family group. If you're ever unsure,
   post there — we'll answer in minutes."*

---

## How to Rehearse Without Scaring

**The practice call** (do it at a calm moment):

> *"Okay Mom, pretend I'm a scammer. I'm calling you in a panic saying I'm
> in jail. What do you do?"*

Walk through it. Praise the correct behavior: *"YES! You asked for the
word! That's exactly right!"*

**Why rehearsal matters:** in a real panic, people don't *think* — they
*react*. Repetition builds the reflex so the right behavior is automatic,
not a judgment call made under stress.

**Re-drill every 6 months.** Put a calendar reminder on your phone.

---

## What NOT To Do

- ❌ **Don't show scary videos of cloned voices.** Creates anxiety without
  building skills.
- ❌ **Don't shame them for almost falling for it.** Shame kills the
  reporting reflex — which is the single most valuable thing they can do.
- ❌ **Don't make the safe word a family joke posted on Facebook.** Public
  defeats the purpose.
- ❌ **Don't overwhelm them with statistics.** One conversation about the
  word is worth ten about the $893M in losses.

---

## If They've Already Been Targeted

- **Reassure first:** *"This is not your fault. This is a crime. Millions
  of smart people have been targeted."*
- **Get the details:** what did the caller say, what number, what did they
  ask for?
- **Run the Recovery Playbook** if money moved — hour 0 matters.
- **Report together:** sit with them and file the IC3 report. It's
  empowering, not scary, when done together.

---

## The Script They Need On Paper

Print this and put it by their phone:

> **If someone calls and says a family member is in trouble and needs
> money:**
>
> 1. Ask for the word.
> 2. If they don't know it — HANG UP.
> 3. Call your family member back on their real number.
> 4. Tell the family group.
> 5. Never send money to someone you can't verify.
