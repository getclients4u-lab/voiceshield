# The Hang-Up Script

**Print this page. Put it by every phone in the house.**

---

## The Script (say it exactly)

> **"I need to verify this. I'm hanging up and calling you back on your
> number. If this is really you, you'll answer. If it's not — goodbye."**

That's it. No arguing. No explaining. No apologizing. Hang up.

---

## What Happens Next (the 60-second rule)

1. **Hang up** — immediately, mid-sentence if needed.
2. **Call the real person** on their known number from your contacts.
   - Answers? Great. Verify it was them. Done.
   - Busy/no answer? Go to step 3.
3. **Post in the family group:**
   *"Possible scam call using [NAME]'s voice. Verify before any money
   moves."*
4. **If money already moved** — do NOT follow the caller's "fix it"
   instructions. Go straight to the 72-Hour Recovery Playbook.

---

## What The Scammer Will Say (and your answer)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'm injured, I can't think!" | "Then you understand why I need the word. What is it?" |
| "There's no time!" | "There's always time to verify. Goodbye." |
| "Don't you recognize my voice?!" | "I recognize clones exist. What's the word?" |
| "I'll get in trouble if you tell anyone!" | "Goodbye." |
| "I'm calling from a friend's phone." | "Call me from your number, or FaceTime me. I'll wait." |
| "You're being paranoid!" | "Goodbye." |

**The rule: every answer redirects to the secret. If they can't produce it,
the conversation is over.**

---

## Why This Works

The scammer's script depends on keeping you **on the line, emotional, and
moving fast**. The Hang-Up Script does the opposite: it ends the call, buys
time, and forces the scammer to either produce family-only knowledge or lose
the mark. They will always choose to lose the mark — there are millions of
other numbers to dial.

**A clone can copy your voice. It cannot copy your family's secrets.**
That gap is the entire product.
