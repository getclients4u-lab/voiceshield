# The "I Already Sent Money" Recovery Playbook

**Read this BEFORE you need it. If money has already moved — start at Hour
0 and don't skip steps.**

---

## Hour 0–1: FREEZE EVERYTHING

**1. Call your bank or financial institution IMMEDIATELY.**
Use the number on the back of your card — NOT any number the caller
provided. Say exactly:

> *"I believe I'm the victim of fraud. I need to freeze the transfer and
> file a claim."*

**2. Wire transfers:** ask to recall/reverse the wire. Banks have a recall
window — every minute counts. Your bank files a recall request through the
receiving bank (Fedwire system).

**3. Gift cards:** call the issuer (Visa/Mastercard/American Express gift
card support) with the card numbers. Unredeemed balances can sometimes be
frozen or refunded.

**4. Crypto:** transfers are irreversible, but report to the exchange used
(Coinbase, etc.) — some can flag the receiving wallet through
chain-analysis partners.

**5. Zelle / CashApp / Venmo:** file a dispute in-app immediately. Then call
support and escalate to the fraud team.

---

## Hour 1–24: DOCUMENT AND REPORT

**6. Preserve everything:**
- Call logs and timestamps
- The cloned voicemail or audio message (do not delete!)
- Text messages
- Transaction receipts / confirmation numbers
- The exact number that called you

**7. Report to the FBI IC3:** https://ic3.gov — file a complaint with all
details. This is required for most bank recovery processes.

**8. Report to the FTC:** https://reportfraud.ftc.gov

**9. Report to local police:** get a case number — banks often require it
before releasing funds.

**10. Notify your bank's fraud department IN WRITING** (email), so there's
a timestamped paper trail.

---

## Day 1–3: ESCALATE AND PROTECT

**11. Escalate at the bank:**
- First agent says no? Ask for the **fraud department**.
- Then the **ombudsman / compliance**.
- Be polite, persistent, and reference your **IC3 complaint number**.

**12. Secure everything else:**
- Change passwords on all financial accounts
- Enable two-factor authentication everywhere
- Freeze your credit (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion)
- Alert the family group — the scammer may re-target with the same script

**13. Talk to a human.** This was a crime against you, not a personal
failure. The emotional toll is real — tell someone you trust.

---

## ⚠️ THE SECOND SCAM WARNING

**The scammer will likely call again** — often pretending to be a "recovery
agent," "refund specialist," or "FBI liaison" who can get your money back
for a fee.

**THAT IS A SECOND SCAM.** Real recovery is free. Report, don't re-engage.
Never pay anyone to recover money.

---

## Recovery Odds (Be Honest With Yourself)

| Method | Reported within hours | Reported after 72h |
|---|---|---|
| Bank wire | Good odds | Poor |
| Credit card chargeback | Good odds | Fair |
| Zelle/Venmo/CashApp | Fair | Poor |
| Gift cards | Fair (if unredeemed) | Very poor |
| Crypto | Very poor | Essentially zero |

**The faster you act, the better your odds. Every hour counts.**
