# 🚩 Voice-Clone Red-Flag Checklist

## The 9 Warning Signs

A call is **AI-generated or fraudulent** if ANY of these appear:

**1. ☎️ The panic hook**
Instant emergency: jail, hospital, lawyer, stranded, "I need bail."

**2. 🤫 Secrecy pressure**
"Don't tell anyone." "Don't call the office." "This stays between us."

**3. 💸 Money in a hurry**
Wire, gift cards, crypto, CashApp, Zelle — "it has to be right now."

**4. ❓ Wrong safe word**
Asked for the family word and got silence, deflection, or a guess.

**5. 🎯 Missed bait question**
Vague answer, changes the subject, "why are you asking that?"

**6. 📵 No video**
Excuses when asked to FaceTime: bad connection, camera broken, "I'm in a
meeting."

**7. 🕵️ Spoofed caller ID**
Number looks right — but calling it back doesn't reach them.

**8. 📻 "The sound is bad"**
The classic clone cover story for audio glitches. Real people don't say
this on every call.

**9. 🧩 The ask is off-pattern**
A request that never happens in your family's real relationship. Your
daughter has never once asked you for money — now it's an emergency?

---

## If 2+ flags are present:

> 🛑 **HANG UP.**
>
> 🔑 **VERIFY** — safe word, bait question, video, call-back.
>
> 🗣️ **CHALLENGE** — "What's the word? What did we eat at Grandma's 80th?"
>
> 👨‍👩‍👧 **ESCALATE** — call the real person, post in the family group,
> and if money moved, run the 72-Hour Recovery Playbook.

---

## The Golden Rule

**Never verify WITH the caller. Verify the caller against what YOU already
know.**

A voice clone can copy sound. It cannot copy secrets. The moment you ask
for the word, the scammer's script is over.
