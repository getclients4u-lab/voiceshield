# Family Safe-Word Builder

**Time: 10 minutes. Do this WITH your family, in person.**

---

## Step 1 — Choose the Safe Word

Rules:
- **Never** posted online, texted, or spoken into voicemail
- Not a pet name, birthday, or address (all public info)
- Memorable enough that a 75-year-old remembers it in a panic
- Two random words beat one predictable word

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Good examples |
|---|---|
| Max (dog's name) | pineapple umbrella |
| 1954 (birth year) | Uncle Roy's hat |
| Smith (last name) | sideways Tuesday |
| Our street name | blue mug |

**Our safe word is:** ____________________________

**Our backup (in case someone forgets):** ____________________________

> **Backup options:** a second word, OR a physical object ("the blue mug in
> the kitchen"), OR a shared memory ("the town where we got engaged").

---

## Step 2 — Choose 3 Bait Questions

Only real family knows these. Write questions whose answers are:
- NOT on social media
- NOT guessable from public records
- Stable over time (not "what did you eat yesterday")

**Bait Q1:** __________________________________________________
**Answer:** __________________________________________________

**Bait Q2:** __________________________________________________
**Answer:** __________________________________________________

**Bait Q3:** __________________________________________________
**Answer:** __________________________________________________

---

## Step 3 — Agree the Hang-Up Script (word for word)

Everyone reads this aloud and agrees:

> *"I need to verify this. I'm hanging up and calling you back on your
> number. If this is really you, you'll answer. If it's not — goodbye."*

## Step 4 — Create the Family Group

- App: ____________________ (WhatsApp / Signal / iMessage / Telegram)
- Group name (innocuous): ____________________
- Rule: **any money request by voice → post in the group within 60 seconds**

---

## Step 5 — The Drill (this week)

Run it once. It takes 3 minutes and it's the difference between a
spreadsheet and a reflex.

1. Pick a family member to play the "scammer."
2. They call another member, doing their best panicked clone impression:
   *"I'm in jail, I need bail money, don't tell anyone!"*
3. The receiver must: ask for the safe word → ask a bait question → if
   failed, deliver the Hang-Up Script → post in the family group.
4. Swap roles. Laugh about it. Do it again in 6 months.

---

## Keep This Sheet

Print it. Put it in a drawer (not on the fridge — the safe word shouldn't be
visible to visitors). The checklist goes on the fridge; this stays private.
